\section*{Statements and declarations}

\paragraph{Data and code availability.} The NHANES 2017--2018 files are public \citep{nchs2020}; the NSW and CPS-1 extracts are those of \citet{dehejia1999} and \citet{lalonde1986}, as distributed by \citet{cunningham2021}. All code, registered predictions and raw results are available at \url{https://github.com/pablotgp/channel-measurement} (archived at \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23639437}{doi:10.5281/zenodo.23639437}). No proprietary data were used.

\paragraph{Ethics.} The study uses only synthetic data and publicly available, de-identified data (the NHANES public-use files and the NSW/CPS-1 extracts). No new data were collected from human subjects, so no ethics approval was required.

\paragraph{Funding and competing interests.} This research received no specific funding. The author declares no competing interests.

\paragraph{Use of generative AI.} This work was carried out with extensive use of an AI assistant (Claude, Anthropic) for drafting, derivations, code and internal review. The author designed and directed the research, ran the computations, checked the results, and takes full responsibility for the content.

\paragraph{Changes from version 1.} Version 2 corrects statements that two further independent reviews found overstated or imprecise. No reported number changes; some statements gain hypotheses or narrower conclusions (notably Theorem~\ref{thm:measurement}(4)), and a remark on the doubly robust estimator with a wrong outcome model under lack of support is added:
\begin{itemize}
  \item The NHANES agreement is reported against both the registered tolerance (7 of 8 comparisons) and $|z|<3$ (4 of 8), with all $z$ values, the H\'ajek results and the finite-population caveat.
  \item The NSW/CPS discussion now gives the estimated probabilities of the two unsupported participants.
  \item Convergence statements are recast as identities of functionals plus consistency.
  \item Several references and hypotheses are corrected.
\end{itemize}
